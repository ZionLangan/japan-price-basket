# Data attribution

The primary price source is the **Statistics Bureau of Japan, Retail Price
Survey (Trend Survey)**, distributed through e-Stat and the Statistics Bureau
website. The starter basket also draws on the **Family Income and Expenditure
Survey**. Dataset records preserve source URLs, observation periods, original
specifications, units, retrieval receipts and native-cell locators. Detailed
sources and transformations are documented in [Data and methodology](docs/DATA.md).

Retained original evidence is byte-identical to its downloaded source. English
labels, normalized quantities, basket totals and estimates are project
transformations. They are not government-published observations or an endorsed
official index. Observations and derived estimates remain distinguishable in
the application and exported results.

Source material remains subject to its applicable terms and any separately
identified rights. Consult the [e-Stat terms of use](https://www.e-stat.go.jp/terms-of-use)
and [Statistics Bureau website-use guidance](https://www.stat.go.jp/info/riyou.html)
before adding or redistributing other material. Retain source attribution with
distributed data and do not imply government endorsement.
